{{AppName}} Privacy Policy

Overview

{{AppName}} is a calculator and estimation tool. We designed it to work without accounts and to keep the figures you enter on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and why.

Your Financial Inputs Stay On Your Device

The income, expense, loan, property and settings figures you enter are stored only locally on your device. We do not transmit them to our servers, we do not sell or share them, and we have no way to see them. Removing the app removes this locally stored data.

Crash and Diagnostic Reporting (Sentry)

To find and fix problems, the app uses Sentry to send crash and diagnostic reports when something goes wrong. These reports can include technical details such as the type of error, a stack trace, the app version, and general device and operating-system information. They are used only to diagnose and improve the app's stability. They are not used to identify you and are not sold. The financial figures you enter are not the purpose of these reports.

Purchases (Google Play Billing / App Store)

If you upgrade to the premium version, the purchase is processed by the app store on your platform (for example, Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store). The store — not us — handles your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of whether a valid purchase exists so it can unlock premium features.

No Accounts and No Advertising Tracking

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in. It does not display third-party advertising and does not use advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the app and in the online version linked below. Please review it periodically.
